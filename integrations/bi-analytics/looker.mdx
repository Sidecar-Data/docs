## Setup

1. Follow [Looker's instructions](https://cloud.google.com/looker/docs/api-auth) to create a Looker **API Client ID** and **Client Secret**.

2. On Sidecar's Looker Integration page, provide the following details:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Base URL** | The domain for your Looker instance (e.g., `https://company_name.cloud.looker.com`) |
| **Client ID** | From Step 1 |
| **Client Secret** | From Step 1 |

3. Click **Test** then **Save**.
