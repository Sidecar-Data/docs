## Integrating the Sidecar Slack App

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Integrations">
    In the Sidecar app, go to the **Integrations** section from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Slack integration">
    Scroll down or search for **Slack**, then click into it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect now**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    You will be redirected to a page where you can confirm the workspace you want to integrate with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow access">
    Click **Allow** to add the Sidecar Slack App to your workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Adding Sidecar to Channels

Adding the Sidecar Slack App to channels enables **Slack thread anchoring** — conversations about data assets are automatically linked in the Sidecar catalog.

<Note>
  Sidecar only supports anchoring for **public channels** to which the Sidecar App has been added.
</Note>

To add Sidecar to a channel:

1. Navigate to the channel in Slack.
2. Click the icon in the top-right that shows channel participants.
3. Click the **Integrations** tab.
4. Click **Add an App**, find the **Sidecar** app, and add it.

Repeat for all channels where you want Sidecar to listen.
